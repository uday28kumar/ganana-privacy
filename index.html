Ganana – Privacy Policy

App: Ganana – Japa Mala Counter (Android, package com.udaykumar.counter)
Effective date: October 10, 2026

Summary

Ganana does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your count and history: the running total, daily counts for the History screen, and your settings (such as auto-reset) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Music you choose: if you use "Add music", you pick an audio file with Android's own file picker. Ganana gets permission to read only that one file, and remembers its location and name on your device so it can play it again. The file is never copied, changed or uploaded. Removing the track in the app releases that permission.
  • Home-screen widgets show the same on-device count.

Ganana does not request internet access, so it cannot send any of this data anywhere.

Android device backup

Ganana allows Android's standard backup. If you have turned on backup for your device (for example Google backup), Android may include Ganana's app data (your counts, history and settings) in your own device backup and restore it to your new phone. This backup is made by Android, is controlled by you in your device settings, and is not accessible to the developer. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Send feedback

The "Send feedback" option opens your own email app with a new message to uday28kumar@gmail.com, pre-filled with the app version and your Android version and device model. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and you can edit or delete any of it first. The developer receives only what you send, along with your email address, and uses it only to reply to you and improve the app.

Permissions

  • Vibration: a gentle vibration when a mala of 108 is complete.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep your chosen music playing with the screen off, with controls in a notification.

Ganana does not request permissions for the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files and storage.

Children

Ganana is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
